### What actually needs recording

The ten diagnostic screen clips are the real gap. F-05 is explicit that these must be
**pre-recorded human voice in the sakhi register, not TTS** — *"a synthetic voice reads as a
government machine and loses the one thing this product has."* The TTS fallback keeps the
सुनिए control functional; it is not the intended experience.

Per Build Manual D4, recording needs no equipment: a phone voice recorder, a quiet room,
the phone 20 cm from the mouth, two takes, keep the better one. Script for each screen is
the question text, then *"पहला विकल्प: …"*, *"दूसरा विकल्प: …"* through the options, so she
can map voice to position.

Two variants of the Q3 clip are needed: variant A with all five statements, and variant B
with statements (a) and (b) omitted, for the Q1=d path.
